Dish Smash Support

A stress-relief game for iPhone. Walk into a tableware shop and break everything in 60 seconds.

How to play

  • Swipe up to throw a dish at the shelves. The 60-second round starts with your first throw.
  • Every piece on the shelves has a price. Your score is the total price of what you break.
  • Tap the house button (bottom left) to change shops. Tap the stack button (bottom right) to change the dish you throw.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock more dishes?

Eight dishes are ready from the start. In the dish menu, a locked dish shows a video badge. Watch one video ad and that dish is unlocked for good on that device.

What does "Remove Ads" do?

It is a one-time purchase that removes the full-screen ads shown between rounds. The optional video ads that unlock dishes remain. You will find it in the shop menu, shown as a button with its price.

I bought "Remove Ads" but still see ads between rounds.

Open the shop menu and tap the restore button (the circular arrow next to the price). Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used for the purchase.

I reinstalled the app or changed phones. What carries over?

"Remove Ads" carries over: use the restore button. Best scores and dishes unlocked with video ads are saved in the app's data on the device, so they are lost when the app is deleted. They usually carry over to a new iPhone if you transfer your data or restore from a backup, but not if you set it up as a new iPhone.

The video did not unlock the dish.

The dish is unlocked only when the video is watched to the end. If no video is available, wait a moment with the dish menu open, or check your internet connection.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking can be changed in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking. In Europe, a gear button in the shop menu reopens the consent choices. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
