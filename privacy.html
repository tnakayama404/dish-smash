Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

In short. Dish Smash has no accounts and no server of its own. Your scores, unlocked dishes, and play records stay on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google's advertising software collects some data from your device to deliver and measure those ads. Purchases are handled by Apple.

1. Who is responsible

Dish Smash ("the app") is published by Takahito Nakayama, an individual developer based in Japan ("we", "us"). You can reach us at tnakayama404@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

The app saves the following on your device only. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.

  • Your best scores for each shop
  • Which dishes you have unlocked
  • Play records, such as how many rounds you have finished (used to space out ads), in which shops you have broken nearly everything, and whether you have seen the how-to-play guide
  • Whether you have bought "Remove Ads"

Your advertising consent choices (where a consent message is shown) are also saved on your device. Google's advertising software reads them and sends them to Google and its advertising partners with ad requests, so that they can follow your choices.

Deleting the app deletes this data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows full-screen ads between rounds and optional video ads that unlock dishes. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and its affiliates, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. When ads are loaded or shown, this SDK collects data directly from your device and sends it to Google. In addition, each time the app starts, Google's consent software in the app asks Google whether a consent message is needed where you are; like any internet request, this reveals your IP address to Google. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

Google handles this data under its own Privacy Policy and under the data protection terms that apply to its advertising services for app publishers, which require it to follow the data protection laws that apply. We do not share data with any third party that does not protect it at least as well as this policy and Apple's App Store rules require.

4. Tracking permission on iOS

Before ads are first shown, iOS may ask whether you allow the app to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose not to allow it, the app cannot access your device's advertising identifier and you will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Consent in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message from Google before it requests any ads. If the app cannot reach Google to check whether the message is needed, it goes by the region set on your iPhone instead: if that region is one of these countries, it requests no ads until the check succeeds. You can accept, decline, or choose in detail how your data is used for advertising. If you decline, you may see ads that are not personalised, or fewer ads. To change your choices later, open the shop menu (the house button) and tap the gear button.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal basis for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising, and for personalised advertising, is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time as described above. Showing ads that are not personalised, measuring how ads perform, audience research, and improving advertising services may instead be based on legitimate interests, where the consent message says so. Those interests are the interests of Google, its advertising partners, and us in paying for a free app with advertising and keeping that advertising working and secure. You can object to this in the detailed options of the same consent message.

6. Purchases

The app offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove Ads". Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app only learns from Apple, on your device, whether the purchase is active.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required for consent to data processing in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Tracking: turn it off in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in Europe: change it from the gear button in the shop menu.
  • Personalised ads from Google: manage them at My Ad Center.
  • Data on your device: delete the app to remove it.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to its processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Apart from emails you send us, we do not hold data that identifies you. The advertising data is held by Google, and we cannot look it up or delete it for you. To make a request about it, use Google's Privacy Help Center. You can also contact us and we will help where we can.

We do not sell personal data for money. Under some US state laws, allowing tracking for personalised ads may count as "sharing" data for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out by turning off tracking as described above.

9. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays there until you delete the app. We do not keep copies. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies, which it describes in How Google retains data we collect.

10. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks and the UK Extension, and it uses standard contractual clauses where needed. Google describes these safeguards on its data transfer frameworks page.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

12. Contact

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the problem you report, and we delete it once it is no longer needed for that (where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in supporting the app). You can ask us to delete it sooner.
